## Files in This Theme

1. `page.tsx` → goes to `frontend/app/page.tsx`
2. `layout.tsx` → goes to `frontend/app/layout.tsx`

---

## Look and Feel

- **Background:** Deep dark navy (slate-950)
- **Primary color:** Emerald Green (emerald-400 / emerald-500)
- **Accent color:** Purple (used in AI Insight chip and gradient endings)
- **Layout:** Split-screen (message on left, spreadsheet preview on right)
- **Style:** Flat Minimal SaaS — clean lines, no heavy gradients
- **Font:** Outfit (modern geometric, premium and clear)

---

## Logo Design

- Custom SVG: rounded emerald square containing
  - A circle (representing the timeline)
  - A subtle 3-by-3 grid inside (representing spreadsheet cells)
  - A small dot at the top of the circle (current time position)
- Wordmark: "Chrono" (white) + "Sheet" (emerald-400)
